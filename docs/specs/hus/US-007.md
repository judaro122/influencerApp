# US-007: Upload Video to YouTube Channel

## As an authenticated user, I want the system to upload my video to my selected YouTube channel so it's published.

## Given / When / Then

- **Given** a video in `UPLOADING` status with successful AI-generated metadata, and a linked YouTube channel belonging to the same tenant.
- **When** the system invokes the YouTube upload pipeline.
- **Then** the video is uploaded to YouTube and the `status` transitions to `PUBLISHED` with a `youtubeVideoId` and `youtubeUrl`.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The video `status` transitions from `UPLOADING` to `PUBLISHED` on success.
2. `VideoEntity.youtube_video_id` and `VideoEntity.youtube_url` are populated on success.
3. A `video-published` Kafka event is emitted (kafka-events.md §4.3).
4. The `YouTubeUploadAdapter` decrypts `access_token_enc` and `refresh_token_enc` using AES/GCM (security-spec §4) transparently before upload.
5. If the `access_token` is expired or near expiry, the adapter refreshes it using the decrypted `refresh_token_enc` before uploading.
6. Upload uses resumable upload via YouTube Data API v3 with streaming from MinIO (no full in-memory buffering).
7. On YouTube upload failure, retry up to 3 times with exponential backoff.
8. On retry exhaustion, `status` transitions to `FAILED`, `errorReason` is populated, and an error event is emitted.
9. If the YouTube quota is exhausted, the circuit breaker opens (50% over 10s, 60s wait) and subsequent uploads are fast-failed.
10. Cross-tenant access is denied: the channel must belong to the requesting tenant.
11. The upload is fire-and-forget; no synchronous wait for YouTube publish (constitution §4.4).

## Out of Scope

- Scheduling or delayed publishing (v1.0, out of scope per constitution §11.3).
- Video transcoding before upload (v1.0, out of scope per constitution §11.1).
